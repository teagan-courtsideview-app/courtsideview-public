What It Does

What can you do with CourtsideView?

CourtsideView brings the jobs that usually get split between paper, group chats, memory, and sideline guesswork into one simple app.

Score the match

Keep points, sets, and timeouts in Scorekeeper. Start a local score-only match without a roster or account.

Track player stats

Use Courtside mode to log kills, digs, blocks, aces, assists, and errors and track who is on court in each rotation.

Stream live with FanView

Send a browser link so fans can follow the live score and watch the match when you broadcast.

Manage rosters

Build teams, keep player profiles handy, and bring the right roster into match day before first serve.

Use Big Mode

Show a larger scoreboard view for scorer tables, benches, and busy gyms where the score needs to be readable fast.

FanView

How to livestream a volleyball match with FanView

FanView is free to broadcast and watch live. Share one link for the live scoreboard and, when you start a broadcast, match video.

1. Publish from the scoring screen

Create a free account or sign in, then start a match in Scorekeeper or Courtside mode. Open FANVIEW and share the published match link.

2. Start live video when you’re ready

Allow camera and microphone access and start the broadcast. Live sharing needs internet. Sharing the score link on its own does not start video or recording.

3. Fans open the link in a browser

Anyone with a valid link can follow the score and watch live video without an app or account. The link expires 15 minutes after the match ends.

Match-day questions

Before your first match

Do fans need an app or account to watch FanView?

No. Anyone with a valid FanView link can follow live scores and watch video when the host is broadcasting. Fans open the link in a browser with internet access, without downloading the app or creating an account.

What is the difference between live scores and live video?

FanView can share scores and match activity without a camera. Live video is optional: a broadcaster must allow camera and microphone access and start the broadcast. Sharing a score link does not automatically start video or recording.

How long does a FanView link work?

The same FanView link stays with the game and expires 15 minutes after the game concludes. A live link is not a permanent video archive.

What does CourtsideView cost?

CourtsideView is free to download. Scorekeeper is free to use, and FanView is free to broadcast and watch live. The broadcaster needs a free account. Viewers only need a valid link and internet access.

Do I need an account to keep score?

Keep score locally in Scorekeeper without an account or roster. Create a free account to track player stats in Courtside mode or publish and broadcast with FanView.

What requires an internet connection?

Local scorekeeping and stored match data work on your device. Publishing and receiving FanView updates, streaming live video, and using connected account features require an internet connection. Offline scoring does not provide live updates to remote viewers.

Which devices can I use?

CourtsideView is available for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Check the linked store listing for compatibility with your device. Fans follow FanView in a modern web browser.
