Quick answer

CourtsideView, sometimes searched as CourtsideViewApp, courtsideviewapp, or Courtside View app, is a volleyball app for iPhone, iPad, and Android.

  • Score volleyball matches from your phone or tablet.
  • Track rotations, rosters, and player-level match context.
  • Share live FanView links so supporters can follow along.

What It Does

One place for volleyball match day.

CourtsideView brings the jobs that usually get split between paper, group chats, memory, and sideline guesswork into one simple app.

Score the match

Run the scoreboard quickly, keep sets moving, and reduce the usual "wait, what is the score?" chaos.

Track player stats

Capture match context for athletes, parents, and coaches without turning every point into a spreadsheet moment.

Share FanView

Send one live link so family and fans can follow the score and match flow from anywhere.

Manage rosters

Build teams, keep player profiles handy, and bring the right roster into match day before first serve.

Use Big Mode

Show a larger scoreboard view for scorer tables, benches, and busy gyms where the score needs to be readable fast.

CourtsideViewApp, courtsideviewapp, or Courtside View app?

If you searched any of those names, you are in the right place. The app is called CourtsideView. The website is courtsideviewapp.com. Families also search for it as CourtsideView app, CourtsideViewApp, courtsideviewapp, or Courtside View app.

The important part: CourtsideView is for volleyball match day. It is not an NBA courtside viewing app, a generic sports streaming app, or a basketball tool. It is built for volleyball scoring, stats, rosters, rotations, and FanView live updates.
