CourtsideView Launches Volleyball Match-Day App for Scoring, Stats, Rosters, and Live Fan Updates

The app helps volleyball families score matches, track player stats, manage rosters, and share live FanView updates from one place.

UNITED STATES, June 18, 2026 - CourtsideView today announced the launch of its volleyball match-day app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. The app gives volleyball families a simple way to score matches, track rotations and player stats, manage rosters, and share live FanView updates with supporters who cannot be in the gym.

CourtsideView was built around the realities of club, school, and tournament volleyball: fast sets, loud gyms, changing lineups, and families asking for score updates from every direction. Instead of forcing scorers to juggle paper notes, group chats, and memory, CourtsideView keeps the match flow in one place.

"CourtsideView was built for the parent who gets handed the scorebook two minutes before first serve and still wants the whole family to feel connected to the match," said Adam Lee Bridegan, founder of CourtsideView. "Our goal is to make volleyball match day easier to score, easier to follow, and a lot more fun."

CourtsideView includes:

- Scorekeeping: Run the scoreboard quickly from a phone or tablet, with match context close at hand.
- Player stats and rotations: Track player-level details, rotation context, and roster information throughout the match.
- Roster management: Build teams and keep player profiles ready before match day.
- FanView sharing: Share a live link so family and fans can follow scores and match updates from anywhere.
- Big Mode scoreboard: Use a larger scoreboard view for scorer tables, benches, and busy gyms.

Availability

CourtsideView is available now for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Volleyball families can download the app at https://www.courtsideviewapp.com/download.

About CourtsideView

CourtsideView is a volleyball match-day app built for families, players, coaches, and fans. The app brings volleyball scorekeeping, player stats, rotations, rosters, Big Mode scoreboard views, and FanView live score sharing into one simple match-day experience.
