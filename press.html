Company background.

CourtsideView is a volleyball-specific match-day app built for the scorer table, the sideline, the parent trying to follow the match, the coach who wants useful context, and the club that needs a practical volleyball workflow.

Core functions

Scorekeeper, Courtside mode, player stats, rotations, team rosters, Club Pages, Coach Hub, FanView, and Big Mode.

Who it serves

Volleyball parents, clubs, coaches, players, scorekeepers, teams, and supporters following from home.

Product direction

CourtsideView is releasing quickly based on feedback from volleyball families, clubs, and coaches.
