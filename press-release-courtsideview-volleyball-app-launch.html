CourtsideView Expands Volleyball App for Clubs, Coaches, and Families

UNITED STATES, June 24, 2026 - CourtsideView today announced an expanded volleyball-specific match-day platform for iPhone, iPad, and Android. The app now brings Scorekeeper, Courtside mode, team rosters, Club Pages, Coach Hub, player stats, Big Mode scoreboard views, and FanView sharing into one volleyball-first workflow.

CourtsideView is building for the part of volleyball that broad sports platforms often miss: the scorer table, the sideline, the parent trying to follow the match, the coach who wants useful context, and the club that needs a simple match-day layer without the cost or weight of a full administration system.

"Volleyball families should not have to choose between paper scorebooks and tools built for someone else's workflow," said Adam Bridegan, co-founder of CourtsideView. "We built CourtsideView to make the scorer table simpler, make the match easier to follow, and give volleyball families a product that feels like it was made for the gym they are actually sitting in."

"As a parent of volleyball players, I know how fast a match can get away from the sideline," said Derek Teasley, co-founder of CourtsideView. "Parents want the score, players want their work to be seen, and family members who cannot be there still want to feel connected. CourtsideView brings those needs together in one match-day experience."

The latest release cycle gives CourtsideView a broader volleyball-first position in a crowded youth sports app market. Rather than forcing families into expensive or overly broad tools, CourtsideView focuses on immense match-day value: fast scoring, player context, rosters, club-facing pages, coach workflows, and family follow-along from one place.

CourtsideView is also releasing quickly based on feedback from volleyball parents, clubs, and coaches. The company expects the platform to keep expanding around real gym workflows, including club visibility, coach tools, team management, scorekeeping, and family sharing.

Scorekeeper and Courtside mode

Run the scoreboard quickly, then use Courtside mode to keep player and parent context connected to the match story.

Rosters, Club Pages, and Coach Hub

Manage team rosters, publish club-facing match context, and give coaches a growing hub for volleyball workflows.

FanView and Big Mode

Share a FanView link for remote supporters and use Big Mode when the gym needs a larger live scoreboard.

Availability

CourtsideView is available now for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Volleyball families can download the app at courtsideviewapp.com/download.

About CourtsideView

CourtsideView is a volleyball-specific match-day app built for families, players, coaches, teams, clubs, and fans. The app brings Scorekeeper, Courtside mode, player stats, rotations, team rosters, Club Pages, Coach Hub, Big Mode scoreboard views, and FanView live score sharing into one simple match-day experience.
