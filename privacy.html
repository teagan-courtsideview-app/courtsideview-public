Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

No accounts No tracking No ads

CourtsideView is a local-first volleyball stat-tracking app for parents and youth coaches. Your match data, roster, and player stats stay on your device. We do not require a sign-up, do not transmit your data to our servers, and do not share or sell information about you or your players.

What we collect

Nothing personal. CourtsideView does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on our servers. There is no account, email, login, or user profile to create.

What stays on your device

  • Roster details you enter (player names, jersey numbers, positions).
  • Match results: scores, sets, rotations, timeouts, and the events you log (kills, digs, blocks, errors, etc.).
  • App preferences: theme choice, team colors, dismissed coaching tips.

All of the above is stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard secure storage (MMKV on iOS and Android). You can wipe everything at any time from Settings → Wipe All Data.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or telemetry on your usage.
  • We don't display ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.
  • We don't access your camera, photos, contacts, or location for any purpose other than what you explicitly initiate inside the app.

Permissions you may see

CourtsideView may request these device permissions only when you take a corresponding action:

  • Photos / camera — when you choose to add a player headshot to your roster. Photos are stored only on your device.
  • Microphone — reserved for a future voice-note coaching feature; not active in v1.0.

You can revoke any of these in your device's Settings app at any time without breaking the app's core stat-tracking features.

Updates over the air

To deliver bug fixes quickly, CourtsideView uses Expo's update service to download new application bundles. The update service receives only the version, build, and runtime identifiers required to deliver the right bundle. No personal information is transmitted.

Children's privacy

CourtsideView is designed for use by adult parents and youth coaches. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The roster and stat data the user (the parent or coach) enters about young athletes stays on their device under their control. If a parent or guardian wants that data removed, they can wipe it via Settings → Wipe All Data; nothing is retained on our servers because nothing is sent to our servers.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be summarized in the app's release notes.
